How a lesson runs

Four steps, every time

Give it a source

A PDF, DOCX, PPTX or text file — or simply type the topic you're stuck on.

It plans the lesson

The material is broken into concepts, sequenced from intro to advanced, and fitted to your time budget.

It teaches on video

Each concept becomes a narrated segment with a lip-synced presenter and a rendered visual board.

It adapts to you

Checkpoint questions are graded live. Miss one and the concept is re-taught with a different analogy.

What's under the hood

Not a chatbot with a play button

Every lesson runs through a retrieval engine, a five-agent teaching loop, and a real video pipeline.

Grounded in your document

Hybrid dense + sparse retrieval with cross-encoder reranking, so explanations cite your chapter rather than drifting into generic facts.

Five specialised agents

A planner, explainer, questioner, evaluator and assessor run a seven-state teaching loop — each one doing a single job well.

Real rendered video

Neural speech, 24 FPS lip-synced visemes, and a visual board carrying equations, graphs, diagrams or code — composited to 1080p.

Misconception diagnosis

Wrong answers are matched against a taxonomy of known misconceptions, so the re-teach targets the actual confusion.

Everything is tracked

Every concept, attempt and answer is stored against your profile — so your dashboard shows what you've actually mastered.

Three languages

English, Hindi and Bengali — including Devanagari and Bengali script parsing, headings and numerals in your source material.
